The problem

Your buyers search in two places now

They still use Google. They also ask ChatGPT and Perplexity which tool to pick. Most SaaS sites are set up for neither.

Traffic that does not convert

The blog ranks for broad terms, but pricing, comparison and use-case pages get little search traffic, so few visitors reach a demo.

AI answers name your competitors

Ask an AI assistant for the best tool in your category. If your brand is missing from the answer, you lose buyers before they visit any site.

Technical issues hide good pages

Blocked crawlers, slow templates, missing schema and weak internal links keep your best pages out of search results and AI answers.

What the audit looks like

Below is the format, filled with illustrative findings for a made-up SaaS. It contains no real client data.

Illustrative example

Audit summary: "ExampleSaaS" (project management tool)

Each finding has a reason, a specific fix and a priority, so your team knows what to do first.

Top findings (sample)
Finding Why it matters Fix Priority
Pricing page has a noindex tag The page buyers search for most cannot appear in Google. Remove noindex, add the page to the sitemap, request indexing. High
No "alternatives" or "vs" pages Buyers comparing tools land on competitor or review-site pages. Publish pages for the top competitors, with honest comparison tables. High
Brand missing from AI answers for category prompts AI assistants recommend other tools when buyers ask for options. Add direct-answer sections, update review profiles, earn mentions on cited list pages. Medium
Feature pages have no schema Search engines and AI models get less context about the product. Add SoftwareApplication, Organization and FAQ schema. Medium
Blog posts do not link to product pages Readers and link authority stay on the blog. Add contextual links from the top posts to the matching feature pages. Low
