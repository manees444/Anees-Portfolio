AEO and GEO, in plain terms

AEO: answer engine optimization

Making your own pages easy for AI systems to quote. That means direct answers near the top, clear definitions, comparison tables, FAQ blocks and schema markup.

GEO: generative engine optimization

Making your brand show up in the other sources AI models read: review sites, "best tools" lists, directories, forums and consistent company data across the web.

Questions

Does AEO replace SEO?

No. AI search tools often draw on pages that already rank well, so strong SEO is the base. AEO and GEO build on it.

Can you guarantee my brand appears in ChatGPT?

No one can. AI answers change by prompt, user and date. What I can do is measure a fixed set of prompts before and after, so you see what changed.
