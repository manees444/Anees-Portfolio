What the audit checks

Technical SEO

  • Indexing, noindex and canonical errors
  • Crawl access for Googlebot and AI crawlers
  • Core Web Vitals on key templates
  • Sitemaps, redirects and broken links
  • Schema markup

Content and pages

  • Coverage of buyer-intent keywords
  • Pricing, comparison, alternatives and use-case pages
  • Pages competing for the same keyword
  • Internal links from blog to product pages
  • Thin or outdated pages

AI-search visibility

  • Whether ChatGPT, Perplexity and AI Overviews mention you for category prompts
  • Which sources they cite instead
  • Pages that need direct answers or FAQ blocks
  • Review and directory profiles

Who it is for

A good fit

  • B2B SaaS with a live product and a marketing site
  • Founders or marketing leads who want more demos or signups from search
  • Teams who suspect AI assistants recommend competitors

What you receive

  • A ranked list of findings, each with a reason and a fix
  • The first pages to create or rewrite
  • An AI-answer check of buyer prompts in your category

Questions

Is the audit really free?

Yes. You get the findings whether or not you hire me.

Do you need access to my Google Search Console?

No. The free audit works from public data. If you share read-only Search Console access, the findings are more precise.

What is the difference between SEO and AEO?

SEO gets your pages ranked in search results. AEO (answer engine optimization) gets your content quoted inside AI answers, such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews. The audit covers both.

Which CMS do you work with?

WordPress, Webflow and Shopify directly. For custom stacks I write tickets your developers can follow.
